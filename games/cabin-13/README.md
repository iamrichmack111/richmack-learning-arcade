# Cabin 13 — First-Person Woods Edition

Cabin 13 is now a first-person survival-horror math game.

## What changed

- First-person camera
- Mouse-look with pointer lock
- Walk directly through a dense forest
- Hundreds of trees with collision
- Ground-level cabin and supply sites
- Flashlight-style darkness
- First-person enemy encounters
- Sprinting
- First-person shooting
- Math still controls food, wood, ammo, medicine, and cash
- Five increasingly difficult nights
- Grade tracking + CSV export

## Controls

- W A S D — walk
- Mouse — look around
- Shift — sprint
- Click — shoot
- E — search a supply site
- F — feed the cabin fire
- H — use medicine

## Run

If the ZIP downloaded to your home folder:

```bash
cd ~
unzip cabin-13-first-person-woods.zip
cd cabin-13-first-person-woods
python3 -m http.server 8130
```

Then open:

```text
http://127.0.0.1:8130
```

Click the game once to capture the mouse for first-person look.
Press Esc to release the mouse.
