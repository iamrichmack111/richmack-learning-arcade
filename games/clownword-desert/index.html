CLOWNWORD
DESERT 2.0

Enter a student name, choose a level, then spell the words carried by clown villains. Every session receives an automatic grade and is saved privately in this browser.

1. MoveUse arrow keys. Hold Shift to sprint. On touch screens, use the direction pad.
2. Focus and spellPress Space or begin typing. Time slows while you spell. Press Enter to cast.
3. Target enemiesEach clown carries a word. Type that exact word to defeat it. Nearby matching targets glow.
4. Seal tentsApproach a tent and spell its gold seal word. Finish its progress to summon a Ringmaster boss.
5. Learn from errorsWrong letters are explained, words are pronounced, and missed words return later for review.
6. Earn a gradeYour grade combines 70% accuracy, 20% completion, and 10% word mastery.

Progress is stored only in this browser with localStorage. No account or internet connection is required.

Enemy types

Runners move quickly with shorter words. Tanks require two correct spells. Mimes hide their word after a few seconds. Jugglers scramble their display while keeping the real word pronounceable. Balloon clowns drift over the desert. Ringmasters are tent bosses with multiple word phases.

Grading and saved records

A session score is calculated as 70% spelling accuracy, 20% percentage of tents sealed, and 10% mastery. Mastery measures how many attempted words were eventually spelled correctly. The report includes the student name, level, grade, accuracy, attempts, correct answers, missed words, tents sealed, bosses defeated, best combo, and elapsed time.

Review system

Missed words are added to a review queue and appear more often later in the same session. The saved student profile also remembers frequently missed words for future sessions.
